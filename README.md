Final-year **Electrical and Electronic Engineering** student at the University of Salford, on track for a First. I've completed a year-long placement at **Cummins Turbo Technologies**, where I worked on actuator testing, validation planning and engineering data analysis.

I'm interested in **test and validation, embedded systems, digital design and power systems**. I enjoy work that goes from building hardware to proving it works.

### Projects

- **[Solar Hosting Capacity of the GB Transmission Network](https://github.com/itunu-ogunbowale/gb-solar-hosting-capacity)**: a Python/pandapower study of how much solar a weak point on a 29-bus model of the GB grid can take. An AC optimal power flow sets an N-1 secure dispatch, then load flow, N-1 contingency and IEC 60909 fault studies give hosting capacities of up to 3000 MW intact and 1000 MW N-1 secure, limited by line and transformer ratings rather than voltage.
- **[Closed-Loop V/F Control of an Induction Motor](https://github.com/itunu-ogunbowale/vf-control-induction-motor)**: a MATLAB/Simulink drive with a PI speed controller and a six-IGBT SPWM inverter. It holds speed at 1000–1400 rpm from no load to full load, with V/F kept within 4% of the 8 V/Hz design value.
- **[Car Park Barrier Controller](https://github.com/itunu-ogunbowale/barrier-control-fsm)**: a VHDL Moore state machine (29 states) for a keypad-controlled car park barrier, handling edge cases like a driver reversing mid-entry. Simulated in Quartus and tested on a DE2-115 FPGA board.
- **[MATLAB Signal Processing Projects](https://github.com/itunu-ogunbowale/matlab-dsp-projects)**: an App Designer tool for audio feature analysis, with a hand-written FFT, approximate entropy, ZCR and RMS, plus a room equalisation study comparing FIR and IIR filters on flatness, phase and processing cost.
- **[Synchronous Sequence Counter on an FPGA](https://github.com/itunu-ogunbowale/fpga-sequence-counter)**: a gate-level 4-bit counter that shows each state in binary, decimal and hex on seven-segment displays. Designed with Karnaugh maps in Quartus, verified in simulation, and tested on a Cyclone IV FPGA.
